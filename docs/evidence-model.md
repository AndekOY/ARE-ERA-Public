# Evidence model: demonstration vocabulary

- **CLAIM:** proposition asserted about a system.
- **SOURCE:** declared origin of evidence; issuer claims and independent evidence are distinguished.
- **EVIDENCE:** artifacts actually supplied and inspectable.
- **TEST:** reproducible check performed on specified artifacts.
- **GAP:** missing artifact, metadata, linkage or verification step.
- **RESULT:** conclusion bounded by evidence and test coverage.
- **UNESTABLISHED:** insufficient evidence to confirm the stated claim within the declared scope; does not mean disproven.

No normative ANDEKS™ authority is asserted by this vocabulary.
