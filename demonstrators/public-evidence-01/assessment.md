# Synthetic evidence assessment

**Object:** Fictional source-to-signal integrity claims. **Question:** Is independent end-to-end integrity established by the illustrative evidence bundle?

1. Identify the claim and the minimum artifacts necessary to reproduce it.
2. Distinguish provider assertions from independently checkable bytes and records.
3. Check whether original artifact identity, canonicalization rule, actual digests, linkage and chain anchor are provided.
4. Record missing material without assuming why it is missing.
5. Limit the result to **UNESTABLISHED** because the fictional bundle is insufficient for independent verification.

**Not tested:** cryptographic digest recomputation, chain continuity, runtime operation, calibration, operational coverage and any real supplier's controls.

**No ANDEKS™ normative decision. No vendor evaluation. No claim of ERA software execution.**
